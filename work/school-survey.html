SaaS · Market expansion · Lead designer

Turning survey samples into the company's best lead source

Role
Lead designer, new site from scratch
Context
Sub-brand site for a new market
Audience
K-12 school and district administrators
Outcome
Live at myschoolsurvey.com

The problem

CustomInsight sells employee engagement and 360 feedback surveys to corporate clients. School climate surveys were one line in that portfolio, and the company wanted to grow it — but a district evaluating a vendor should not have to work out what a product built for them is doing inside a site built for HR directors.

That meant a dedicated site for the education market, competing against larger, better-known K-12 providers. Administrators there spend public money on something they cannot touch first, which makes credibility a design problem before it is a marketing one.

My role

I built the site from scratch — no existing property, no template, no visual direction for the sub-brand. I researched the competitive set, ran sessions with the client to pin down goals, audience and messaging, and worked directly with their content lead so copy and structure were designed together rather than in sequence.

Design decisions

Structure the site the way administrators buy

School leaders evaluate in a predictable order: what does it do, who is it for, what does it cost, how do I start. The site answers in that order, with a conversion moment at each decision point instead of one contact page at the end. If someone is convinced on page two, the site should let them act on page two.

Gate the one thing prospects most wanted

The sample surveys were the highest-intent content on the site. Anyone asking to see the actual questions is not browsing. I designed them as a gated download — a short form in exchange for the full set — which turned the site's most valuable page into its primary lead source and qualified the leads at the same time.

The judgment was in the calibration. Gate too little and you capture nothing; gate too much and you insult a busy administrator. Enough of the sample stays visible to establish credibility; the complete set is what asks for the introduction.

The page people wanted most became the page that earned the conversation.

Close the credibility gap with structure

Reading as an established provider is not about looking expensive. It comes from answering questions before they are asked. I translated the strategy into wireframes, high-fidelity design and a reusable Figma component system, so new sections get built from existing parts — which is what keeps a site coherent after the designer leaves.

Outcome

Live at myschoolsurvey.com. The gated samples give the company a steady stream of contacts who have already shown real intent, and the structure supports lead generation by design rather than working against it.

What I'd carry into a larger team

Conversion design and brand design are not separate crafts. The gated download worked because the surrounding experience made the company feel worth trusting with an email address.
